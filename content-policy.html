Submission guidelines

Content Policy

This policy applies to anyone submitting an event or a news/blog story to Trifoss via our submission forms.

What we accept

Events and stories relevant to Trifoss's mission and community. Submissions should be accurate, respectful, and your own work (or work you have the rights to share).

What we don't accept

  • Defamatory, hateful, discriminatory, or illegal content
  • Images or text you do not own or have permission to use
  • Spam, unrelated advertising, or promotional content unrelated to Trifoss

Review process

Every submission passes through two rounds of moderator review before it appears on the site. We may edit submissions for clarity or length, reject submissions that don't meet this policy, or remove published content later if a concern is raised.

If your submission is rejected, you'll receive an email with the specific reason. Since our forms don't support editing a submission after the fact, you're welcome to revise and submit a fresh entry any time.

Rights

By submitting content, you confirm you own or have permission to share it, and you agree it may be published on the Trifoss website and used in related Trifoss communications.
