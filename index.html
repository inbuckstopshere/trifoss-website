About Trifoss

Trifoss — the RI Fellowship on Sustainable Solutions — is an international fellowship of dedicated professionals committed to advancing sustainable development through collective knowledge, cross-border collaboration, and on-the-ground projects. We bring together engineers, educators, entrepreneurs, healthcare workers, environmentalists, and civic leaders who share one conviction: that meaningful change is possible when people of goodwill work together across boundaries.

Trifoss was founded by a group of like-minded service professionals who recognised that the world's most pressing challenges — climate change, inequality, food insecurity, loss of biodiversity — do not respect national borders, and therefore cannot be solved by any one nation alone. What started as informal conversations between concerned members across continents has grown into a structured international fellowship with a shared agenda, a diverse community, and a growing portfolio of collaborative projects.

Vision & Mission

What we're working toward, and how

Vision

A world where people and planet thrive together — where every community, regardless of geography, income, or circumstance, has access to clean resources, resilient infrastructure, quality education, and the opportunity to participate fully in a just and sustainable economy.

Mission

To catalyse sustainable solutions through fellowship, knowledge, and action — convening a global community of passionate individuals, facilitating the exchange of expertise and best practices, and co-creating projects that generate lasting positive impact in communities around the world.
