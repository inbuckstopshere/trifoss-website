What we collect

When you join Trifoss, submit an event or news story, sign up for the newsletter, or contact us, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, and any content or images you submit.

How we use it

We use this information to process membership sign-ups, review and publish submitted content, send newsletter updates (only to those who sign up), and respond to enquiries. Submitted content and images may be published publicly on this website once approved by a moderator.

How it's stored

Submissions are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive, accessible only to Trifoss administrators and moderators.

Donations

Donation payments are processed directly by Razorpay (domestic) or Stripe (international). Trifoss does not directly handle or store your card details.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your submitted content, unsubscribe from the newsletter, or request what personal data we hold about you, by contacting us (see Contact page).
